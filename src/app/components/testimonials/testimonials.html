ABOUT ADCUT STUDIOS

AdCut Studios was started by Arun Chelani — an Filmmaker known for MITRATA - Friendship's Lovestory.
Has always been drawn to what happens between the frames.

Over the years, we’ve had the opportunity to work with 150+ brands across commercials, films and digital content. Our work moves between production and post-production — shoot, edit, colour, sound, music, VFX and finishing.

We’re a small, hands-on studio, and we like being close to the work. From the first frame to the final export, we stay involved in the process and the details that shape how a film feels.

For us, filmmaking is less about getting from A to B, and more about finding the moments in between.
