ABOUT ARUN

Arun is a Mumbai and international based senior video editor, colorist, and visual storyteller. On completing his film production studies, he pursued specialized masterclasses in Cinematic Editing and Color Science from DaVinci Resolve Academy.

He has worked with renowned production houses, directors, and top-tier global creators. He has contributed as Lead Commercial Editor on high-impact brand campaigns and commercial films across Asia & Europe.

Arun has been independently editing since 2020 and has cut more than 100 commercials and music films for global brands including Netflix, Tata, Amazon, ICICI, Starbucks, Instagram & Meta to name a few.
